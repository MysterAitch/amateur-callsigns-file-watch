scope & disclaimer

Proof-of-concept mirror; not affiliated with or endorsed by Ofcom, and not authoritative – Ofcom’s own register is. The value here is continuity: a decade of snapshots Ofcom does not itself publish as a series. Presence in an availability list means offered, not licensed. Absence is read scope-aware: missing from a partial publication is not evidence. The record flags what looks inconsistent and adjudicates nothing.
