sightings

Where and when it was seen

One row per archived dataset, oldest first: register snapshots, availability lists and other callsign-bearing disclosures. Absence is read scope-aware — a callsign missing from a partial or undeclared-scope publication is not evidence of anything, and even a declared-complete export is intent, not verified fact. Presence in an availability list means the callsign was offered as available (not licensed) at that date.
