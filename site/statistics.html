Parsed records by prefix series and Regional Secondary Locator (latest dataset). Every reference series and RSL letter is shown — all-dot rows and columns are the sparsity signal, not noise; · means zero; ⚠ marks locators observed in the data but absent from reference data. Excluded records are counted beneath the table.
