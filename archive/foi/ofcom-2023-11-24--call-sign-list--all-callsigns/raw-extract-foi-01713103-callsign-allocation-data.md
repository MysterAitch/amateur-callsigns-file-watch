Reference: 01713103
5 December 2023
Information Requests

**Freedom of Information request: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request for information concerning callsign allocation data.
This request was received on 8 November 2023 and we have considered it under
the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

You asked (Andrew Robinson):

*I'm requesting information in regards to the current up to date allocated
amateur radio callsigns, including all levels foundation, intermediate, full
displayed clearly in an excel or Google sheets format, indicating current
allocated and available callsigns, with clear definition if they are available
or taken and if marked reserved, include the explanation as to what that means.*

**Our response** — We are able to provide data as of the date a report is
generated from our systems, in this case 24 November 2023. Please find attached
a list of all UK amateur radio callsigns as of 24 November 2023, showing the
callsign, the type of licence each callsign is associated with, the current
status of each callsign and the date on which each callsign attained that
status, where this information is available.

Regarding the request for an explanation as to what reserved means: a callsign
would be marked as reserved following a surrender of a licence. Callsigns are
generally reserved for two years following surrender.

If you have any further queries, then please send them to
information.requests@ofcom.org.uk, quoting the reference number above.

Yours sincerely,

Information Requests
