## Overview

- **Asked**: a list of all unallocated UK amateur radio callsigns.
- **Provided**: in full — `Amateur Available Call signs 16 Apr.xlsx`, three
  worksheets (extents indicative, from declared dimensions):
  - `Foundation` — ~6,712 rows × cols A:H, available-pool
  - `Intermediate` — ~8,646 rows × cols A:H, available-pool
  - `Full` — ~7,230 rows × cols A:H, available-pool
- **Withheld**: nothing. The response letter is inline on the thread
  (reproduced below); the message carries exactly one attachment.
- **Significance**: part of the 2015 available-pool run (compare
  wdtk-247308, seven weeks earlier); a thread annotation by the wdtk-247308
  requester cross-links the two — community awareness of the repeat-request
  cadence question was live at the time.
