## Overview

- **Asked**: how many full-licence two-letter-suffix callsigns ("T
  numbers", G\*XX) were re-issued to a different person since 2010 — listed
  with re-issue dates, clubs excluded.
- **Provided**: `list-re-issue-amateur-radio-call-signs.xlsx` — one data
  sheet, **113 rows × 2 cols (`Original Start Date | Call Sign
  T-Number`)**, callsigns disclosed (e.g. G7DMN, G8KW, G0WAB…).
- **Withheld**: nothing, but three methodology caveats are on the record:
  - **Same-name ambiguity**: Ofcom "cannot easily tell" returning
    hobbyists from same-name family bequests — both are included.
  - **Duplicate accounts existed "because of changes in our systems"**,
    with "a data cleansing exercise to merge many of these duplicate
    accounts" — a system-migration artefact admission (timeline evidence
    for the 2016 transition, issue #129).
  - **Semantics**: the sheet's "original start date" is the start date of
    the licence *under which the callsign was re-issued* — i.e. the
    re-issue event date, not the callsign's first-ever issue.
- **Significance**: the first `issuance-events` dataset in the archive —
  actual re-issue events with dates, complementing the policy-side
  signposts of wdtk-251507 (heritage re-issue policy) and quantifying
  ~113 two-letter re-issues over ~7.9 years.
