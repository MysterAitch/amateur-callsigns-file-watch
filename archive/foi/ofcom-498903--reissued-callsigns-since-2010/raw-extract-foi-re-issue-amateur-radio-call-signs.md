Reference: 498903
22 December 2017
Jerin John, Information Rights Adviser

**Freedom of Information: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request for information where you asked about re-issue
of Amateur Radio Call Signs.

This was received by Ofcom on 26 November and it has been considered under
the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

You asked:

*Under the freedom of information act I would like to know the following.*

*Since 2010 until the date of this request how many full amateur radio call
signs (T numbers) have been re- issued to another licensed person, i.e the
call sign has been issued originally to a person which has subsequently
been re-issued to another with their person or family person.*

*Please list these call signs and include the date of re-issue in year
order.*

*Typical call signs of interest will be in the format of G\*XX, (\* being a
number).*

*Please do not include club call signs.*

In order to provide you with the list you have asked for, we have made
certain assumptions.

**Where the previous and current call sign holders have the same name, we
cannot easily tell whether these are call signs issued to returning
hobbyists or happen to bequeath their call sign to a family member of the
same name. These have been included.**

**There may also be instances where the same call sign has been assigned to
the same person on 2 duplicate accounts. This is because of changes in our
systems. We have undertaken a data cleansing exercise to merge many of
these duplicate accounts.**

Please find attached the list of Amateur Radio call signs re-issued since
2010, with the call sign and date of re-issue. **Please note the original
start date refers to the start date of the licence under which the call
sign was re-issued.**

If you have any queries, then please contact
information.requests@ofcom.org.uk. Please remember to quote the reference
number above in any future communications.

Yours sincerely

Jerin John
