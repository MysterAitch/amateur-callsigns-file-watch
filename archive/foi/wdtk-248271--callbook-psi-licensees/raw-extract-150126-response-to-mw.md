Reference: 1-277622422
26 January 2015
Information Requests, Secretariat

**Freedom of Information: Right to know request**

Dear Mr Witton,

Thank you for your request for information about the licensees of Ofcom's
amateur radio call book data. Your request was received on 14 January 2015.

You asked for the following information:

*This is a freedom of information request. Please send me the list of
licencees of the Amateur Radio Call book data under Re-use of Public Sector
Information Regulations 2000. To be clear - I want a list of the licensees
of the call book data not a list of amateur radio licencees nor the call
book data itself.*

I am writing to advise that Ofcom has completed its search.

**Anyone is able to apply for a licence to have the call-book data using the
application form attached to this letter, as long as the information is used
for the purposes described. However, our records show only one request for a
copy of the application form has ever been received and there are no records
to show that an application was completed. As a result Ofcom has no
information to supply you as it is not aware of anyone who has such a
licence**

If you have any further requests do please get in touch. Please quote the
reference number above in any future correspondence about this request, as
this will help us to deal with your query more quickly.

Yours sincerely,

Information Requests
