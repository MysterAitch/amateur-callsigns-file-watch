## Section 1 — Re-use of Public Sector Information Regulations 2000

The Re-use of Public Sector Information Regulations 2005 ('PSI') implement a
European Commission Directive that encourages the re-use of information
supplied by a public body for purposes other than they were originally
intended. Whilst access legislations such as Freedom of Information and
Environmental Information Regulations seek to promote transparency and
accountability in public authorities, the PSI recognise that public
information has a value and encourages the commercial exploitation of this.
The regulations apply to any recorded information in which the public
authority holds the copyright.

Ofcom complies with the Regulations on the Re-use of Public Section
Information and we encourage the re-use of the information that we produce.

Our approach is to be open and transparent and to treat all applications to
re-use in a fair and non-discriminatory way.

If you wish to re-use information that we produce that please complete the
application form below and return it to us. A copy of the terms of the
licence has been included with this form. **Please note that the information
gathered by Ofcom is only for the purposes of producing a call sign book,
and will not be licensed for any other purpose.**

In accordance with PSI Regulations we will aim to respond to all
applications within twenty working days.
