## Overview

- **Asked**: the list of licensees of the amateur radio call-book data
  under the Re-use of Public Sector Information Regulations — explicitly
  *not* the call-book data itself, nor a list of amateur licensees.
- **Provided**: the answer that **no such licensees exist** — "our records
  show only one request for a copy of the application form has ever been
  received and there are no records to show that an application was
  completed… Ofcom… is not aware of anyone who has such a licence" — plus
  the application form and licence terms themselves
  (`Amateur Radio Callbook PSI 1.pdf`, publication date May 2009).
- **Withheld**: nothing (there was nothing to withhold).
- **Significance**: documents that the **formal PSI route to callsign
  call-book data existed from May 2009 and was never used** — the licence
  offered callsign + name + mailing address, free, one-year term,
  callbook-purposes-only, with licensee names to be listed publicly. The
  pre-open-data commercial licensing regime was, on the record, an empty
  set as of January 2015; the separate RSGB call-book arrangement
  (documented in the UKGWA-recovered RSGB licensing set) was evidently not
  under this PSI scheme. Pairs with the requester-side context of the
  open-data origin story (wdtk-224333, wdtk-247308).
