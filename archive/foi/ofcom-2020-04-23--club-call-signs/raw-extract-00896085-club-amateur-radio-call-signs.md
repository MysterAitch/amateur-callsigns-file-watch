Reference: 00896085
30 April 2020
Emma Leech, Information Rights Adviser

**Freedom of Information: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request for information in relation to club amateur radio
call signs.

We received this request on 8 April 2020 and have considered it under the
Freedom of Information Act 2000.

You asked:

*Please provide me with a list (spreadsheet would do) of ALL CLUB amateur radio
call signs that have been issued to date.*

**Our response** — I have attached a list of all the club amateur radio call
signs that we hold. This includes all licences which are live or were cancelled
after 1 April 2014. We do not hold records of any licences which were cancelled
before this date.

I hope this information is helpful. If you have any further queries, then please
send them to information.requests@ofcom.org.uk, quoting the reference number
above.

Yours sincerely,

Emma Leech
