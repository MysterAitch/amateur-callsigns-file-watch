**Freedom of Information request**

Dear Mr Eckersley

Thank you for your information requests and your subsequent request for
internal reviews. First let me apologise for the confusion over your
different requests and reviews – this was our fault and we will seek to be as
clear as possible in our responses in future.

To this end, I am responding to all your outstanding requests and internal
reviews in one letter. All of the case reference numbers to which this letter
refers are set out above. For the sake of clarity I have set out below the
chronology of your requests and reviews and Ofcom's responses

12 November 2014 [1-273972981] - request for call signs issued in the last 10
years out of sequence.

18 November 2014 [1-274238044] – request for call signs issued in last 10
years from series' first allocations pre-WW2

19 December 2014 – request sent to Julia Snape, asking for an internal review

Based on the above chronology, I will conduct and internal review of the

response to your other request [1-276355280]. I will not be conducting an
internal review of your 19 December request [1-276355280], as we haven't

response, you can then request the review.

**1-276484403 - Internal review of 1-273972981 and 1-274238044**

Following a review of your request and the information we provided, I have
found that **we did not answer your question regarding call signs issued out
of sequence correctly**. In our original response we cited Section 12 of the
Freedom of Information Act, where the costs of compliance would exceed the
appropriate limit. This conclusion was based on an initial assessment of our
system and your request and **following further inquiry it has turned out to
not be accurate**.

.On the subject of callsigns issued out of sequence, **we have listed all call
signs issued since 1st December 2006 and arranged them in order of the date
of issue. Having examined this data, there does not appear to any strict
sequencing of the call-signs. This is because licensees have the ability to
request their own personalised callsign upon application. This appears to be
the more standard practice for customers.**

With regards your request for call signs issued in the last 10 years, which
were first issue before WW2; I have reviewed our response to you and I am
pleased to say that the information is now attached on the first tab of
Annex A

**1-276355280 – Request for information**

You made 3 further requests on 19 December; I will answer them 1 at a time.

- Request for call signs out of sequence, since 2007 – as I have mentioned
  above, **there is not sequence in which call signs are issued, so Ofcom
  does not hold this information**
- Request for call signs first issued since 2007, which were first issues
  pre-WW2 – **it is our understanding that all call signs issued before
  World War 2, were in the format 'GXaa'**. This information is included in
  the first tab of Annex A.
- Request for the column headings of information held on radio amateurs –
  the column headings on our database of amateur radio licenses are attached
  in the second tab in Annex A. **It should be noted that not all these
  fields are populated for each licensee.**

I hope you find this response satisfactory and that it clears up any
misunderstanding about your previous requests.

Yours sincerely

Graham Howell
