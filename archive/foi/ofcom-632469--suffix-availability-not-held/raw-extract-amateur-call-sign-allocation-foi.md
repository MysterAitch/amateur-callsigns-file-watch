Reference: 632469
30 October 2018
Catriona Lawrence, Information Rights Adviser

**Freedom of Information: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request for information where you asked for Amateur
Radio available call signs.

This was received by Ofcom on 27 October 2018 and it has been considered
under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (the Act).

You asked:

*I am applying for my UK amateur intermediate call sign and I would like to
see what call sign suffix are available to me to use.*

We do not hold lists of available call signs. Instead, our licensing system
generates them on demand. **The system is designed to generate any
three-letter combination for the suffix of a call sign, excluding those
combinations already on issue and those which have been deemed unsuitable.**

If you have any queries, then please contact
information.requests@ofcom.org.uk. Please remember to quote the reference
number above in any future communications.

Yours sincerely,

Catriona Lawrence
