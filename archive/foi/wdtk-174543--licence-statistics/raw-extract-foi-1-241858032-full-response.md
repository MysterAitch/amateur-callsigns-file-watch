## Observation (extractor's note, not document content)

The stated answer — **3,723 individuals holding one or more licences** — is
implausible against the 81,801 licences then in force (it would imply ~22
licences per person). A plausible interpretation is that the underlying
query counted individuals holding *more than one* licence; the letter
nevertheless says what it says, and no correction or follow-up appears on
the request thread. Treat the figure as anomalous-as-published.
