## Overview

- **Asked**: a list of all unallocated UK amateur radio callsigns.
- **Provided**: in full — `Available Call Signs 21 01 16.xlsx`, "an up to
  date list of available amateur radio call signs as at 21 January 2016";
  a single data worksheet (extents indicative, from declared dimensions):
  - `Sheet1` — ~20,737 rows × cols A:J, available-pool (all licence levels
    in ONE sheet — the three-sheet layout of every earlier file in the
    series is gone; two extra columns appear: `Call Sign Application #`,
    `MMSI Application #`)
  - `Sheet2`, `Sheet3` — empty
- **Withheld**: nothing. The response letter is inline on the thread
  (reproduced below); the message carries exactly one attachment.
- **Significance**: **the last successful available-list in the series.**
  The same requester's repeat in October 2016 was answered "not held" —
  the licensing-system change ended the availability-list era (Ofcom later
  stating, in the 2021 Adam Dean internal review, that callsigns are
  generated on demand). The single-sheet 10-column layout marks the final
  evolution of the export format; UKGWA asset 83577
  (`1-313474335_available_call_signs.xlsx`) shares this exact shape and is
  a suspected sibling of this or a near-contemporaneous export.
