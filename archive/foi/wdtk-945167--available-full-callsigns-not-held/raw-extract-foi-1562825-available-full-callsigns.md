Reference: 1562825
1 March 2023
Gloria Akinyemi, Information Rights Adviser

**Freedom of Information: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request for information about amateur radio licence
callsigns which are available.

We received this request on 2 February 2023 and have considered it under the
Freedom of Information Act 2000.

You asked:

*In order to apply for a Amateur Radio Full Callsign that is available on
request as per the OFCOM site ('Call signs using formerly issued prefixes
are not available via the licensing portal and must be requested on a paper
application form (a fee is payable) Please could you send me a list of those
formally issued callsigns, which are available for me to request, the fee and
the process by which I request.*

**Ofcom does not hold the data requested** — it does not hold a discrete list
of all amateur radio callsigns that may have been issued in the past. The
letter signposts the published amateur callsign list (which shows
Allocated/Reserved as unavailable and Available as requestable, though it is
not comprehensive), and explains:

- any callsign not listed may be requested if it is in a valid format for the
  level of licence applied for;
- the default prefixes are M7 (Foundation), 20 (Intermediate) and M0 (Full);
- a callsign with a default prefix can be requested online with no fee;
- a callsign with a different prefix must be requested on a paper application
  form, with a one-off fee of GBP 20.

If you have any queries, then please contact information.requests@ofcom.org.uk,
quoting the reference number above.

Yours sincerely,

Gloria Akinyemi
