## Overview

- **Asked**: a list of the formerly-issued Full callsigns that Ofcom's site
  says are available only on paper application (with the fee and the process),
  because callsigns using formerly-issued prefixes are not offered through the
  online licensing portal.
- **Provided**: no such list — Ofcom **does not hold a discrete list of all
  amateur radio callsigns that may have been issued in the past**. The reply
  signposts the published amateur callsign list and explains the request
  route: any validly-formatted callsign not shown as unavailable may be
  requested, online for a default-prefix callsign (no fee) or on a paper form
  for a non-default prefix (a one-off GBP 20 fee). See the
  [raw extract](raw-extract-foi-1562825-available-full-callsigns.md).
- **Significance**: another instance of the recurring not-held answer to
  "give me the available callsigns" — the register lists what is *unavailable*
  (Allocated/Reserved) and a non-comprehensive Available set, but no
  enumerated pool of requestable formerly-issued callsigns exists, because the
  system generates callsigns on demand rather than drawing from a stored list.
