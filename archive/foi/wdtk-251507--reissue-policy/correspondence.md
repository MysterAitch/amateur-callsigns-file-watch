## Overview

- **Asked**: (1) all internal policy documents / practice notes used when
  considering whether a qualified person may be issued an "old" callsign
  (pre-war-format G + single digit + two letters, e.g. G3CO); (2) the
  reasons each of the last 20 such applicants was granted one — callsigns
  and personal details redacted at the requester's own suggestion.
- **Provided**: in full — `policy for old call signs.pdf` (the policy /
  practice notes) and `applicants old call signs.pdf` (anonymised reasons
  for the last 20 heritage issuances).
- **Withheld**: nothing (the request was framed to avoid personal-data
  refusal grounds).
- **Significance**: the on-the-record policy for heritage/two-letter
  callsign re-issue under the pre-2016 regime, plus a sample of actual
  issuance rationales; the request text also records the observed issuance
  rate (6–9 heritage callsigns per year) and cites the requester's earlier
  reference chain (1-273972981, 1-274238044, 1-276355280). Companion entry:
  wdtk-238892 (out-of-sequence counts + pre-war series list).
