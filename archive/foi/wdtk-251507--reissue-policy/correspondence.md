## Overview

- **Asked**: (1) all internal policy documents / practice notes used when
  considering whether a qualified person may be issued an "old" callsign
  (pre-war-format G + single digit + two letters, e.g. G3CO); (2) the
  reasons each of the last 20 such applicants was granted one — callsigns
  and personal details redacted at the requester's own suggestion.
- **Provided** (see the raw extracts for both documents): no *internal*
  policy documents — the response letter points to Ofcom's **published**
  web pages (call-sign transfer guidance, Special Contest Call signs
  application, call-sign formats, two FAQs) and to the amateur-licensing
  consultation (paras 2.32–2.41) and Statement (paras 3.17–3.22); plus
  `applicants old call signs.pdf` — a 20-row table of heritage-callsign
  transfers 2012-07-30 → 2015-01-28 in which **the callsigns themselves are
  disclosed** (G8JC, G2CP, G3LX, G2NF, G8VL, G5XV, G5LK, G6YM, G2SR, G5TO,
  G3NR, G4RS, G6BD, G3WM, G8WY, G2UG, G2HW, G4ZU, G6OI, G8WQ), each with
  licence product, start date, and the uniform reason "Letter of consent
  provided for transfer".
- **Withheld**: holder names/titles, under s.40 (absolute exemption) — the
  callsigns were not withheld despite the requester having offered that
  redaction.
- **Significance**: per-callsign heritage-transfer evidence for the
  pre-2016 regime — including five G2-series transfers; the covering
  letter's statement that all rows are club licences is contradicted by
  the table itself (three are Amateur Full: G2NF, G3NR, G6BD); the letter
  also records the constant-identity policy rationale and the
  natural-person club-licensing model. The request text records the
  observed issuance rate (6–9 heritage callsigns per year) and cites the
  requester's earlier reference chain (1-273972981, 1-274238044,
  1-276355280). Companion entry: wdtk-238892.
