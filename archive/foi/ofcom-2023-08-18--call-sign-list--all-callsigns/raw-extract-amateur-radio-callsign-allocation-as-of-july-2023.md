Reference: 01649066
21 August 2023
Information Requests

**Freedom of Information request: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request for information concerning Amateur Radio Callsign
allocation. Your request was received on 24 July 2023 and we have considered it
under the Freedom of Information Act 2000.

You asked:

*I would like to request a list of all UK amateur radio callsigns previously
allocated as of July 2023, showing the type of the licence, the current status
(e.g. allocated, reserved), and, where possible, the date of allocation for
each callsign.*

**Our response** — We are able to provide data as of the date a report is
generated from our systems, in this case 18 August 2023. Please find attached a
list of all UK amateur radio callsigns as of 18 August 2023, showing the
callsign, the type of licence each callsign is associated with, the current
status of each callsign and the date on which each callsign attained that
status, where this information is available.

If you have any further queries, then please send them to
information.requests@ofcom.org.uk, quoting the reference number above.

Yours sincerely,

Information Requests
