Reference: 01667041
2 October 2023
Information Requests

**Freedom of Information request: Right to know request**

Thank you for your request concerning a list of amateur radio call signs where
the format doesn't fit the licence class. Your request was received on 4
September 2023 and we have considered it under the Freedom of Information Act
2000 ('the FOI Act').

You asked:

*On your FOI response dated 21/08/23 reference 01649066 you have provided a
list of current issued amateur radio call signs and licence class.*

*M5SHA is listed as a foundation licence but takes the format of a full amateur
licence holder. This goes against your own licence class policy.*

*Please provide a list of all call signs similar in nature where the call sign
format issued does not fit the licence class issued.*

**Our response** — Ofcom noted Section 84 of the FOI Act (which defines
"information" as "information recorded in any form") and ICO guidance that a
public authority is not required to *create* information to answer a request.
Ofcom is **unable to provide the information requested as it does not record it
in this way**. Some callsign and licence-class information is available on the
Ofcom website under "Amateur radio callsign allocation", where the list may be
browsed to identify the information sought.

If you have any further queries, then please send them to
information.requests@ofcom.org.uk, quoting the reference number above.

Yours sincerely,

Information Requests
