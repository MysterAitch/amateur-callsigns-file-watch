## Overview

- **Asked**: a list of all issued callsigns whose *format* does not match their
  *licence class*. The request builds directly on the held 18 August 2023
  register snapshot — "on your FOI response dated 21/08/23 reference 01649066
  you have provided a list of current issued amateur radio call signs and
  licence class" — and gives **M5SHA** as its example: listed there as a
  Foundation licence, but carrying a Full-format callsign.
- **Provided**: no list — a **Section 84 refusal**. Ofcom does not record which
  callsigns have a format inconsistent with their class, and is not required to
  create that information to answer a request; the reply signposts the
  published amateur-callsign-allocation page. See the
  [raw extract](raw-extract-foi-01667041-licence-class-format-mismatch.md).
- **Significance**: the request is a downstream observation about the held
  [`ofcom-2023-08-18`](../ofcom-2023-08-18--call-sign-list--all-callsigns/)
  snapshot rather than a new dataset. The M5SHA format/class-mismatch
  observation is verified from the letter and recorded here; the refusal
  confirms Ofcom holds no enumerated list of such mismatches.
