F1 refined — the chosen stacked hub, re-sequenced with a deliberate rhythm

The #712 review converged on F1's pure stacked order with adjustments: the action chips and stats sit below the search band, the orientation line under the stats, and two hairline rules mark the section breaks into the record band and the role tabs. A deliberate three-step spacing scale (tight within a band, a moderate card gap, a generous section break) gives each band its own breathing room, and the role-tab cards now carry an explicit clickable affordance.

  • Candidate F1 refined

    Stacked hub with a deliberate rhythm

    F1's top-to-bottom order given the F4-refinement's spacing scale, so every band reads as a distinct group.

F1 final — implementation-ready, with the holdings map

The refined F1 hub taken through the independent critique to an implementation-ready candidate: the dots timeline is replaced by the corpus-wide holdings map (the built site's publisher-page component, reused across all ~65 datasets, every cell deep-linking to its own dataset page and a keyboard skip past the grid); a real <h1> roots the outline; the footer is the build stamp alone; the search hero runs full width; the orientation sentence precedes the figures; and the surprise card is lifted above the map.
