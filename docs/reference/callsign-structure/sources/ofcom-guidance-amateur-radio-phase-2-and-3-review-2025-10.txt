1. Amateur Radio Review
    Update

We have now implemented Phases 2 and 3 of our Amateur Radio licensing review. These changes
are designed to modernise the licensing framework and improve flexibility for licensees. The updates
include:

         · Intermediate M8 and M9 go live-- new Intermediate licensees will now be able to
               choose an M8 or M9 call sign. Existing licensee can change their current 2 series call
               sign to the corresponding M series. For 20 call sign holders we have reserved the
               corresponding M8 call sign and 21 the M9) via the online portal.

         · Ability to change call sign-- all licensees can now propose a new call sign of their
               choice via the online portal, once every 5 years. The system will check whether the
               requested call sign is available (i.e. not currently in use). Once a call sign is
               surrendered or revoked, it will remain unavailable for assignment for 5 years.

         · Introduction of a single personal licence -going forward licensees will only be able
               to hold a single personal licence. If progressing to the next level your previous lower
               level licence(s) will be revoked. For existing licensees not progressing we will an
               update later concerning the revocation process.

         · Enhanced online licensing tools-- we have made it easier to manage your licences,
               select a wider range of call signs and validate information online. Licensees that fail
               to revalidate their licence every 5 years will see their licence revoked.

         · Updated rules for Special Event stations(SES)-- we have liberalisedthe use of SES.
               The new SES will allow call signs to a maximum 6 characters in length, in the
               format`GBdccccca' (d = digit other than 3 or 7, c = digit or letter, a = letter).
               Licensees will be able to choose a range of SES call signs, as long as they start `GB'.
               As a result of these changes we have removed the Special SES Notice of Variation.

Information on the Radio Amateur Framework is also available here.
